# Errby motion research

19 September 2026. Scope: `/` public landing; findings combine project requirements, current official documentation, live observations and explicitly labelled design judgments. No conversion or cognitive-performance uplift is claimed. [Live-reference inventory](live-reference-observations.md) records actual interactions; historical screenshots are composition references only. [Motion specification](motion-spec.md) holds selected values; [verification](verification.md) distinguishes lab measurements from untested field behaviour.

## Evidence matrix

Every R01–R60 aspect is investigated. “High” means confidence in a scoped documented fact, not validation by target learners.
