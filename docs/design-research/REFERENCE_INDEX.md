# Errby landing-page reference index

Access date for every record: **17 September 2026**. Public pages only; no accounts created, paid access purchased, or restrictions bypassed. A reference's own claims are not verified outcomes for that product or for Errby. Recognition is not conversion evidence.

**16 selected references:** seven detailed live-page studies, three supporting live sites, three award listings, two concept shots, and Mobbin's accessible public page. Mobbin's protected flow library is excluded from the evidence. Additional failed attempts are listed separately, not counted as successful studies.

Browser desktop viewport: **1280 × 720 CSS px**. Mobile comparison viewport: **390 × 844 CSS px**. Desktop raster captures are commonly 1265 × 712 because browser capture excludes the scrollbar/surface edge; this is not a second viewport. Full-page captures have variable heights. These are research screenshots, not assets licensed for use on Errby's landing page.

## Access and capture limitations

- [Ramotion](https://www.ramotion.com/): browser showed security verification. Stopped; no challenge bypass, no design observations. Instrument and COLLINS supplied the agency alternatives.
- [ReWords AI listing](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/rewords-ai): found in education index; detail fetch failed and then returned a non-retryable tool access error. Excluded from findings.
- [11sight listing](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/11sight-video-call-platform): web-reader failure; excluded from detailed findings.
- Awwwards' AI category fetch failed; the successfully inspected Catalyze AI listing covers AI/SaaS instead. Search indices and award snapshots sometimes differ from browser-rendered content; observed rendered pages take precedence for visual claims.
- Duolingo and COLLINS full-page screenshots stretched viewport-relative hero areas. Their ordinary viewport and detail captures are authoritative for layout. The full-page files remain capture artifacts and must not be measured as true whitespace. Canva/Notion DOM measurements returned invalid 0/1px viewport data, so those numeric readings were discarded.
- Screenshots freeze motion. Only observed changing media/headlines and exposed playback controls are described. Animation durations, hover timing and reduced-motion compliance of reference sites were not audited.
- No account onboarding, child testing, conversion analytics or controlled usability study was performed. No private design connector was needed or accessed.
